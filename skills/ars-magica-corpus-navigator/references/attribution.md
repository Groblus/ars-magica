## Modifications

The source corpus converts and manually corrects open-licensed Ars Magica books into Markdown. The `Groblus/ars-magica` fork further:

- curates a Definitive Edition and 5th Edition subset;
- generates heading-aware chunks, tables of contents, structured rule extracts, and a SQLite FTS index;
- packages the allowed source Markdown and generated data as a self-contained agent skill;
- changes local paths and lookup scripts so installed copies can verify their citations.
